DATA SCOPE

What NetScope can see

Socket and listening-port data is collected from the local Linux host. Failed SSH logins are read from the local system journal or authentication log. To monitor traffic between other machines, connect a mirrored switch port (SPAN) or network TAP and deploy a dedicated passive sensor. NetScope does not currently claim visibility into every packet on the LAN.
